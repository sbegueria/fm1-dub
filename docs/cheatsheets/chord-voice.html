7 · Vary the Chord page's KNOB 4

A trim on Form's Vary (sheet 11). Centred: 0 the notch.
0
As Form's Vary (the pieces' own: 0 to -45).
Right
The stab pattern turns more often (every two bars it may move a step, gain or lose a stab); more throws.
Left
Stiller: fewer turns and throws, fewer stabs and bars left out (never more than Form's Vary leaves out).
HOLD
Far left (the word inside the ring): never, whatever Form says. Every stab of the pattern played, no throws but a fill's last.
