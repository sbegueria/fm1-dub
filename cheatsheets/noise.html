Noise

SAVE: a crackle groove rung on the chord, and the air under it, as of 1.4.0 (build 671d9be). FM-1 firmware.

1 · What it is

Not a bed of hiss: a voice, played like the drums. It plays only while the music does, fading in and out over half a second.
The groove
Hits on a two-bar pattern of semicorcheas (16ths), spread evenly, turned, two nudged a step off, now and then a quiet ghost. Break and Thin phrases: fewer.
Rung
Each hit a short burst that strikes three resonators on the chord's root, third and fifth, low (E1 to Eb2), retuned at each hit; then a band-pass takes the top off.
The air
A quiet hiss under it, and now and then a dry vinyl click.
The duck
It dips on every beat, deeper than the music, and swells between.
Sends
Into the plate, a little into the echo; more while a stab is thrown.
