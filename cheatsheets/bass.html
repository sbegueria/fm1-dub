Bass

LFO: a sub on the chord's root, its groove held a phrase at a time, as of 1.4.0 (build 671d9be). FM-1 firmware.

1 · What it is

One note at a time on the chord's root, low (E1 to Eb2), on a groove of semicorcheas (16ths). It slides briefly from note to note.
The plan
A phrase plays A A A B A A A B (card 8). Which notes rest, which are the fifth, the octave note: drawn once a phrase, the same every A bar.
Octave note
Now and then one note an octave up (never the bar's first), the same place each bar: more often as Vary rises.
Borrowed groove
Rarely, at a phrase, Groove steps to a neighbour, later back: as the square of the bass's amount (at 100: 7 phrases in 400; Hold: never). Lock rests it.
Dry
No echo in any piece (its strip's Echo 0); some plate. The duck pushes it deeper and lets go slower: it pumps.

7 · Level, strip, LFO held

OCT- / OCT+
On a bass page: its level, 5 a press; both together, muted or back.
The strip
SEL, white 3 (A3) held: Level, Echo, Plate, Filter. Black 3 (A#3): a tap solo, held a throw, driven so it carries in the echo (sheet 2).
LFO held
0.5 s, a nudge: Bite, Shape, Gate, Glide, Fifth a step (Ratio, Feedback on an FM voice). 1.5 s, a shake: another voice and a neighbouring groove, every value a big step.
