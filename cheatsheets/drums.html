2 · How the kick plays a shape a phrase

Its note
The key's root, F1 to B1 (44-62 Hz); below F, its fifth. One note a piece.
Held
A shape for 2 to 6 phrases (fewer as Vary rises), then drawn again round the knob: further from it with Vary up. On Four, always four in Full and Groove.
Sections
A Thin a step sparser; out in a Break and a fill's second half. Back whole after a Break. Motion's Breathes: out a bar or two now and then (sheet 11).
Opening
Four for two phrases, then down to the knob over six (till it's turned).
