Arrangement

SEL: the desk, the dub moves and the effects under your fingers, as of 1.4.0 (build 671d9be). FM-1 firmware.

1 · The parts white 1–6 · F3–D4

The chord, pings, bass, kick, hats (with the rim and the clap) and noise: the meters' rows, top to bottom.
A tap
Mute, and back: its level kept. The same mutes as OCT- + OCT+ on a layer's page.
Held
A third of a second, or a knob turned: its channel strip on the knobs until you let go (then no mute).

2 · Solos and throws black 1–5 · F#3–D#4

Each the part of the white key on its left; D#4 the kick's (the noise has none).
A tap
Solo: the others go quiet, their echoes ringing out. Several play together. Again: let go.
Held
Past a third of a second, a throw: the part slammed into the echo at its own level, whatever its sends, the feedback climbing so the repeats stack. Let go: they ring on (and no solo).
Chord
Every stab into the echo.
Pings · hats · kick
As they are (the hats with the rim and the clap).
Bass
Driven, so its harmonics carry in the echo.

3 · The moves white 9–12 · G4–C5, held

As long as the key is held: each sweeps in, and eases back as you let go.
Open
The filter pushed open: the music thins and brightens over the kick.
Shut
The filter pushed shut: the music under water, the kick still pumping.
Drop
The floor out: the kick and the bass gone while held.
Echo
The echo pushed: everything into it, feeding back.
Open and Shut push on top of SELECT's filter, over about 0.4 s.

4 · The effects white 13–16 · D5–G5, held

Four of the effects grid's (REC held), one key each; they play with the grid's.
Time x2
The echo's time doubled (halved if the tape is too short), gliding there and back.
Freeze
The cloud stops listening, its grains thick and brought forward.
Tape stop
The echo's tape slowing to a quarter, its pitch diving.
Stutter
A semicorchea (16th) of the mix caught and looped.

5 · The sirens black 8–11 · A#4–F#5

A tap: a short one, about a bar. Held: slow from where it is, till let go, on its page. One at a time, into the echo. Whoop off the key on purpose; the rest on its root, fifth, octave.

7 · Lock, and the lights

LockSEL held
Half a second, any page; again, off. Each phrase keeps the kick, the groove and the plan (the sections and chords move on). LOCK in red in the header; not saved.
Lit
A key that does something; a part's while you hear it.
Dark
A move, effect or throw held, a siren sounding; a part muted or soloed out; the gap, F#4, G#4.
Blinking
A soloed black key.

Leaving

A page's button leaves (HOME: to Form): the keys are the scale again. Mutes and solos stay; the moves, throws, effects and sirens let go. SEL brings it back.

The screen

The desk's meters, a row a part and the master; the white notch its level. A muted row dark, soloed-out rows dimmed. A card says what each key did.
