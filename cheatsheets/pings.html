DUB-1

Pings

ARP: four loops of FM blips sliding against each other, as of 1.4.0 (build 671d9be). FM-1 firmware.

1 · The loops

Four loops, 13, 17, 23 and 29 corcheas (8ths) long, each with a few notes of the chord. They slide against each other and line up again only after hours.
Notes
Mostly the chord's (now and then an octave up); one in four a passing note, the scale's 2nd, 4th or 6th above the chord's degree.
Each loop
Its own register, its own place in the stereo field, its own shade of tone and length.
A pass
At each loop's end: notes added or taken to Density; with Vary, a note moved or changed, or the next pass sat out.
Sends
Mostly into the echo; the dry against it each piece's own.

3 · The voices ALGORITHM, a pings page

Every ping voice is FM. Round, a click a voice; each piece keeps its own.
Blip
The ping: its modulator's ratio set by the piece's Ping tone. 2-op.
Marimba
A wooden bar's fourth partial (4:1). 2-op.
Glass
A long, bright ring (3.5:1), two pairs: algorithm 5.
Wood
A knock (1.41:1), short. 2-op.
Drop
The pitch falling from an octave up. 2-op.

6 · Level, strip, ARP held

OCT- / OCT+
On a pings page: their level, 5 a press; both together, muted or back.
The strip
SEL, white 2 (G3) held: Level, Echo, Plate, Filter. Black 2 (G#3): a tap solo, held a throw (sheet 2).
ARP held
0.5 s, a nudge: Tone, Ratio, Length, Bend (Upper, Feedback too); now and then a note of one loop. 1.5 s, a shake: another voice, every value a big step.

7 · The algorithms Algo, on every FM page

The 4-operator synths' eight, and a ninth of two operators. Teal, on the line: the carriers, heard; above, each modulator over the one it modulates; the loop: op 4's feedback (Feedback). Here Ratio moves op 2 and Upper ops 3 and 4; on the chord's and the bass's FM pages Ratio is ops 3 and 4.
