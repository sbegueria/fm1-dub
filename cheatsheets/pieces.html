DUB-1

The pieces

PRESETS: the eight recipes, each after one record, as of 1.4.0 (build 671d9be). FM-1 firmware.
A piece
A recipe for the whole machine: key, tempo, the voices, the groove, the space, how much it changes. Each remembers how you left it; GLO held 1.5 s puts it back to its recipe.
These values
The recipes at build 671d9be: each piece's Vary, trims, Motion and Drift measured on the albums it's after. Vary on its scale: 50 the classics' middle.

Basic F Dorian · 122after Basic Channel, BCD (1995)

Its idea
The source: the loop held, short saw stabs into a clean echo, almost no hats, narrow and dark, the kick on the floor.
Sound
Saws · Blip · Sub on Offbeat · Tight, Four · Vinyl · echo 1/8. (corchea con puntillo, dotted 8th)
Form
Vary 50 · Motion 12 (Breathes) · Drift 50 · Change 64 bars · Hits 8 (Shaker) · Pulse first
Try
Hits past 40 (Offbeats): the open hat on the "ands", Maurizio's way. Motion to 40 for its first sections.

Steppers D Dorian · 126after Maurizio, M-Series (1997)

Its idea
A frozen loop: one chord held, short stabs, a dotted bass, the open hat on the four "ands", real swing, clean.
Sound
Saws · Marimba · Punch on Dotted · Punch, Four · Vinyl · echo 1/8. · Swing 59%
Form
Vary 58 (drums -33) · Motion 8 (Breathes) · Drift 35 · Change Hold · Hits 41 (Offbeats) · Pulse
Try
Swing (the Key page) down to 0, then back: how much it carries. Change to 32 bars to let the chord walk.

Concrete C pent. minor · 136after Monolake, Hongkong (1997)

Sound
Noise · Glass · Pluck on Roller (Density 80) · Punch, Four · Vinyl · echo 1/8.
Form

Pressure E Phrygian · 126after Porter Ricks, Biokinetics (1996)

Its idea
It never breaks: the kick and the bass forward, one chord held, a wide bed of tape noise under it.
Sound
Tonewheel · Wood · Growl on Offbeat · Deep, Four · Tape · echo 1/8. · Duck 70
Form
Vary 76 (chord, pings, drums -45: the stabs nearly held; bass -3; noise -14) · Motion 8 (Breathes) · Drift 50 · Change Hold · Hits 39 (Open hat) · Pulse
Try
The noise's Density (SAVE) up from 18: the bed turns to a groove. The bass's Bite for more growl.

How to read a card

Sound: the chord's voice · the pings' · the bass's voice on its groove · the kit, the kick's shape · the noise's character · the echo's time. Form: Form's Vary (and the trims not 0), Motion and its zone, Drift, Change, Hits and its zone, the song's Kind.

Every piece

Endless at first (Song mode, GLO again: the player's). In a song, 32 bars of intro, 160 of main, 32 of outro: 7:20 at 122 (6:35 to 7:43; sheet 12). The bass dry; the kick on the key's root (below F, its fifth). None of the recipes in Breaks.
